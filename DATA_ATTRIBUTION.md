# Data Attribution

SkillPulse AI uses the following external dataset for local research and reproducible
derived analysis:

- **Title:** Indonesian Data & Analytics Jobs in Jobstreet
- **Creator:** Rafli Rizkya Sakti Nugraha
- **Source:** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/raflirizkya/indonesian-data-and-analytics-jobs-in-jobstreet

SkillPulse AI modified the source through identity validation, deterministic sampling,
normalization, taxonomy mapping, human annotation, extraction evaluation, and aggregate
analysis. The creator, Kaggle, JobStreet, and listed employers do not endorse SkillPulse
AI.

The raw CSV is not included in public Git by default. Use `scripts/fetch_dataset.ps1` to
download the declared Kaggle version and verify it against SHA-256
`a857603f6d8a2b0344f4a4f00747e037ecc4ca3aa6b760800560ad4fe906887c`.

This attribution covers the Kaggle dataset license. Additional rights or platform terms
may apply to individual third-party job-posting text; derived aggregate outputs and
synthetic examples are therefore preferred for the public portfolio.
